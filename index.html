Your channels

Uploads, auto-upload and Go Live use the active channel unless you pick another one. To add a channel, sign in with its Google account (or pick its brand account) when Google asks.

Sign in through another Google Cloud project's client JSON. That channel then uses that project's test users and its own daily upload limit.
⚠ “Access blocked … has not completed the Google verification process” (Error 403)?
  1. Open Google Cloud Console → Google Auth Platform → Audience with the account that made the JSON.
  2. Under Test users press + Add users, type the Gmail of the new channel, and Save.
  3. Press ＋ Add channel again. Google shows “Google hasn't verified this app” → Continue.

While the app is in Testing, Google ends its logins after 7 days and the channel has to be connected again. To keep it connected, press Publish app on the same page; on the warning screen choose Advanced → Go to (app name).

Using a separate JSON? Add the channel's Gmail as a test user in that project too. Choose the Desktop app type when you create it.
